# Adding the Lab and Floris's Calendar

To add the Lab calendar and Floris's calendar:
Go to your Outlook calendar, then:
"Add calendar"
"Add from directory"
Select your account to search from
Enter Floris's email address: floris.delange@donders.ru.nl OR the PBL lab calendars email address: predictivebrain@donders.ru.nl
"Add"
This should give you access to the Floris's and the PBL calendar respectively. Once you are there, you can see the free spots in Floris’ week and from your own calendar create a meeting with Floris as an invited person, effectively booking the spot on his agenda as tentative. Lastly, if the time proposed works for Floris, you will get a confirmation of it.
